Renewals and forfeiture

Students who held a room in a past session can ask to renew it. Approving creates their application for the current session and keeps the room held; the room is re-allocated only after the hostel fee is paid. After the renewal deadline, rooms of students who did not ask are forfeited: the allocation is released (history is kept) and the bed space is freed.
